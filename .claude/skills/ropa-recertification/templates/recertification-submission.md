# Owner-facing recap (use at the end of a check-in)

Plain English only. No legal terms. Replace the braces.

---

**Thanks, {ownerFirstName}. Here is what we covered for "{recordName}".**

{One sentence: what the process is for.}

**What you confirmed is still right**
- {Topic}: {one short phrase, e.g. "Your team still runs this"}
- {Topic}: {…}

**What you told us has changed** *(only if there are changes)*
- {Tool/Data/Retention/etc.}: ~~{old}~~ → **{new}** — {their reason in a few words}
- Add: {vendor or system or data type}
- Remove: {vendor or system or data type}

**Skipped**: {anything they chose not to apply, or "nothing"}

**Anything you want the analyst to know?** {ownerNote or "No extra note"}

**What happens next**
A privacy analyst will look at this and may message you with a question. Nothing changes in the
official record until they approve it. Once they do, your next check-in is set for
{nextReviewDate, if known, else "the usual review date"}.

---

If nothing changed, replace the middle sections with:
"You confirmed everything is still accurate, so there are no changes to review."

If the check-in was paused for an ownership change, use instead:
"I have paused this check-in and will reach out to {newOwnerName}'s team. Nothing has been sent
to the analyst."
